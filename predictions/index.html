  • Odds → probability. Bookmaker decimal odds are converted to implied probabilities, then the bookmaker's margin is stripped out so Home/Draw/Away add up to 100%.
  • Suggested outcome. The favourite — unless the game is incredibly tight (no side reaches a 40% win chance), when a draw is suggested instead. A confidence label is shown (Strong ≥55%, Lean 40–55%, Toss-up otherwise).
  • Suggested score. A fixed anchor tuned for the scoring system: 2-0 when it's almost a walkover, 1-1 for a draw, and for a close win 1-0 if few goals are expected (back the exact score) or 2-1 if more goals are expected (a safer middle-ground). Final-round group games where a draw sends both teams through get a small nudge toward the draw.
  • Predict-by tag. Each game shows when to lodge your pick (2 days before kickoff). You'll also get one email digest covering all games entering that window.
  • Full schedule. Every round is listed in date order. Knockout fixtures show as "teams & odds to be confirmed" until the group stage decides them and bookmakers post prices.
  • Suggestions are a data-driven nudge from the market — not a guarantee.
